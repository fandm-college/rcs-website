## Data Management

Almost every federal proposal now asks you to explain how you'll store, protect, and share the data your project creates. Our guide covers what changed for NIH and NSF plans in 2026, the kinds of data RCS can't host, how F&M stores and protects research data, and short, copy-ready language you can drop into your plan.

[Read the Data Management and Sharing Plans guide](../data-management-plans/)

We'd also be happy to read your draft before you submit. [Email us](mailto:dorc@fandm.edu) and allow about a week.
